10. DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

People and purpose first
Start by understanding the audience, information needs, business goals, and desired outcome before choosing the structure or visual direction.

Clarity in visual design
Use typography, spacing, contrast, alignment, and hierarchy to guide attention and make information easier to understand.

Responsive and accessible by default
Design for phones, tablets, and larger displays while considering semantic structure, readable content, keyboard access, and inclusive interaction patterns.

Maintainable implementation
Use semantic HTML, organized CSS, reusable components, consistent patterns, and understandable JavaScript to make products easier to maintain and extend.

Performance and quality
Consider browser behavior, performance, responsive states, accessibility, content density, and implementation quality throughout development.

Dependable delivery
Clarify expectations, organize work into manageable stages, communicate progress, review the finished product, incorporate feedback, and deliver complete work with a clear handoff.

11. WORKFLOW
01 — DISCOVER
Understand requirements, users, constraints, content, competitive context, goals, and success criteria.

02 — PLAN
Organize content, information architecture, navigation, user journeys, wireframes, and responsive states.

03 — DESIGN
Develop visual hierarchy through typography, spacing, color systems, components, and consistent design rules.

04 — BUILD
Translate designs into responsive and interactive frontend experiences using semantic structure and maintainable implementation patterns.

05 — VALIDATE
Test across devices and browsers, review keyboard interaction and focus states, consider accessibility and performance, and address edge cases.

06 — IMPROVE
Review the finished product, incorporate feedback, deploy, maintain, and plan future improvements.
